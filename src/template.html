Signal

Speed = codes on screen × swaps per second × bytes per code. Watch the receiver's measured KB/s and step up until it stops climbing: the camera is the limit, not the screen. Grid mode needs the receiver's built-in scanner (Chrome or Edge). Bigger codes need the camera closer and sharper.
