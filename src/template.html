Every code is a fresh random mix of the file's blocks, so the receiver can join late, miss codes, and still finish. Click the codes for fullscreen.

Signal

Speed = codes on screen × swaps per second × bytes per code. Watch the receiver's measured KB/s and step up until it stops climbing: the camera is the limit, not the screen. Grid mode needs the receiver's built-in scanner (Chrome or Edge). Bigger codes need the camera closer and sharper.

Camera

Point it at the sending screen. Fill the frame with the codes, hold steady, avoid glare. Codes you miss are re-mixed and sent again, so you can start late and still get the whole file.
